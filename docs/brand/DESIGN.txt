SYNTIDE — SIGNAL CONVERGENCE

Concept
Amber synthetic trajectories approach the shape of a teal real-data reference.
The compact mark distills this relationship into three flowing wave strokes.
The companion sculpture expands it into a dimensional field of signals.
These are conceptual illustrations, not measured data or claims of equivalence.

Palette
Real signal: #087F8C
Synthetic signals: #BD6A20
Deep ink: #192E3B
Light paper: #F4F7F8
The Blender materials use brighter illuminated variants of these colors.

build_logo.sh — PhotoCraft composition and export commands

Typography
Inter SemiBold (600). Wordmark is editable in PhotoCraft.
Keep clear space around the mark and preserve its proportions.

Workflow
The symbol was generated using Codex's built-in image_gen tool (not CLI fallback).
The wordmark and cover were assembled with the installed PhotoCraft CLI.
The companion image was modeled and rendered using installed Blender 5.1.1.
The raster mark is embedded in the editable logo; the wordmark remains live type.

Regeneration
Run Blender in background with generate_convergence.py, then build_logo.sh.
The script uses the installed PhotoCraft CLI and local Inter fonts.
The exact final symbol generation prompt follows.

FINAL LOGO PROMPT
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: finished original logo symbol for Syntide, synthetic time-series research software.
Design an exceptionally simple and memorable compact waveform mark: three smooth continuous rounded strokes flow from left to right in a single rising-and-falling wave. Initially offset and independent, the two warm amber synthetic traces gently approach a thicker rich teal reference trace toward their right ends, leaving precise negative space. The overall silhouette subtly suggests a lowercase flowing s/tidal wave, but remains ONE coherent compact symbol. No separate top/bottom swooshes.
Visual style: calm modern Swiss identity design, geometric monoline construction, generous deliberate gaps, perfectly smooth round caps, flat solid colors, uniform-weight strokes, understated sophisticated proportions. Much simpler and less aggressive than a sports logo. No sharp blade tips. One teal stroke #087F8C; two amber strokes #BD6A20. Pure crisp vector-like artwork without any shading, gradients, textured edges or noise.
Centered square canvas, symbol uses 72 percent of width and about 52 percent of height with roomy transparent margins. No text at all, no mockup, no 3D, no shadows, no circles, no badge, no arrow, no infinity loop, no heartbeat spike, no axes. Truly transparent background. One logo, no variations sheet.
